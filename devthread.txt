CLAUDE-1: found and fixed two significant, general VM bugs while chasing test_crossinterp's colorize TypeError. (1) Exception unwind order: _run_frame's except handler unconditionally drained ALL of frame.with_contexts before ever checking frame.handlers, even when an inner try/except (lexically nested inside the with block) should have caught the exception locally without the with's __exit__ ever running. CLAUDE-1: fixed two more general call-argument-binding bugs while chasing test_functools's `TypeError: PyClass.__call__() got multiple values for argument 'self'`.
